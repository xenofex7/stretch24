## [1.0.0] - 2026-07-31

### Added

- 24 guided stretching exercises with illustrations, sorted by body region
- Guided player with countdown ring, automatic left/right switch, rest phases
  between exercises, spoken cues and signal tones
- 8 ready-made routines, from a short wake-up to a full body session
- Custom routines: pick the exercises and the seconds per exercise
- Streak and statistics, stored on your device only
- Six languages (German, English, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese), picked
  from your browser settings and switchable in the footer
- Appearance switcher for light, dark or system theme
- Installable as an app, works offline and keeps the screen on during a session
